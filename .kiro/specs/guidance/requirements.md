# Guidance library and Ask Afterglow — Requirements

Product description 7.18 (a curated, cited library, readable in the Night Shell and by voice) and 7.20 ("AI may ... phrase deterministic lookups with citations"). EARS notation.

- **GL-01** THE SYSTEM SHALL carry a library of guides (food in the fridge and freezer; insulin and cold medicines; carbon monoxide; keeping warm; heat; generators and inverters; safe water; light and candles; phones; medical devices), each as ordered steps, the signs that mean "get help now", and the publisher and title of every source it follows.
- **GL-02** THE SYSTEM SHALL add no medical judgement of its own: where guidance depends on the product (insulin), it SHALL say the label and the pharmacist decide.
- **GL-03** WHEN the person asks a question THE SYSTEM SHALL match it on the phone, offline, against the library's passages (rarer words weigh more; a guide's own title and keywords count) and answer with the best guide's most relevant passages, always including that guide's danger signs, and its sources.
- **GL-04** IF no passage matches THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL say the question is outside the library and SHALL NOT answer it.
- **GL-05** THE SYSTEM SHALL read any guide or answer aloud on request, naming its sources.
- **GL-06** THE SYSTEM SHALL say the library is guidance, not medical advice.
- **GL-08** WHERE no pharmacist-approved table is loaded, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the library's insulin guide, with its FDA sources, folded under the medicine refusal, and SHALL NOT turn it into a clock. (2026-11-27, Claude Code.)
